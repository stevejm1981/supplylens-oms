# The demo script

> `Nine stops, about eight minutes`

1. **Dashboard**: stock value at landed cost; point at the tour card.
2. **PO-0001**: one container, two suppliers' POs; freight by weight, duty by value, handling by quantity, all reconciling exactly. This is the their previous inventory platform-replacement pitch in one screen.
3. **Stock → GRD-FIREPIT-01**: the tranche table shows base £38.50 becoming ~£53 landed. Costs are explainable, line by line.
4. **PO-0003 (draft)**: place it, receive it into a warehouse, watch Stock and the averages move live. Then allocate a fresh invoice to it and refresh: they move again.
5. **Goods-In Station**: PO-0004 sits part received; scan the ginger barcode `5060871330158`, give the 180 bottles a lot and a best-before, receive, and show the PO flip to Received with three deliveries listed. Then open **DRK-ELDER-750**: the Batches card shows both lots in FEFO order.
6. **Bundles → BDL-BBQ-STARTER**: the constraint row explains bundle availability at a glance.
7. **Sales Orders → new**: pick a customer, watch salesperson and warehouse fill themselves, add a bundle line, create.
8. **Fulfil it**: Create despatch → Mark picked → Despatch with a tracking number: stock deducts (components, not the bundle) and margin columns appear at landed cost. Show SO-0012 for a split order (one shipment gone, one still picking). Then **Create invoice**; optionally **Create credit** with restock ticked: stock comes back and Reports nets it off.
9. **Carrier Invoices → PW-INV-30977**: what the carrier charged versus what we accrued at despatch, variance by consignment, and on SO-0001 the True margin line, revenue minus COGS minus carriage, the number legacy platforms cannot produce.
10. **Reports**: 30-day sales, margin % at landed cost, top 5 products, salesperson leaderboard. The buy side and sell side sharing one cost truth.
11. **Channels → Very**: nudge the threshold from 5 to 50, watch SKUs flip to OOS in the preview, put it back, download the CSV.
