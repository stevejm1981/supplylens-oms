# Dashboard

> `/dashboard`

Your landing page: an eight-tile overview of the whole operation, twelve months of expense vs profit, the latest purchase orders, and a guided tour. Every number derives live from the ledger and documents, there is no stored counter to drift.

1. **Read the overview.** *Inventory value @ landed cost* (with total units on hand), *Products* (physical SKUs, bundles, channels), *Sales orders* (total and open), *Customers*, *Out of stock* (physical products at zero or oversold), *Units sold, 12 months* (despatched eaches straight off the movement ledger), *Open purchase orders*, and *Profit, 12 months* (invoiced net minus COGS and carriage). Every card is a link to its register.
2. **Read the ebb and flow.** The *Expense vs profit* chart pairs two bars per calendar month for the last twelve: expenses (COGS at landed cost plus carriage cost to serve) in grey, profit (invoiced revenue net of credits, minus those costs) in teal, losses in red below the axis. The seed tells a full trading year: the Christmas homeware peak in November and December, the January to March trough, and the summer garden build peaking in June. Hover any bar for the exact figures; despatch or invoice something and the current month moves.
3. **Scan Recent purchase orders.** Status pills mirror the flow: `Draft` → `Placed` → `Received`. Click any reference to open it.
4. **Use “The two-minute tour”.** Four cards that jump straight to the demo moments: start here when showing the app to someone new.

> **Note:** Where the year of history comes from The seed includes twelve months of settled, paid trading history (orders SO-0019 onward, restocked by PO-0005 at the same landed costs) purely so the chart has a real shape. Today's stock levels, averages, and the demo walkthrough numbers are untouched by it.
